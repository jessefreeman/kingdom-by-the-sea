🌊 Wang Tile Template Generator

What is this? This tool generates a Wang tile template for auto-tiling systems. Wang tiles use edge patterns to seamlessly connect adjacent tiles. Each tile has connections on its North, East, South, and West edges.

What is a 47-Tile Wang Template?

8-Neighbor Auto-Tiling
Unlike simple 4-direction tiling, this considers all 8 surrounding tiles (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) for smooth transitions.
47 Essential Patterns
These 47 tiles cover all the essential patterns needed for seamless auto-tiling without needing all 256 possible combinations.
Inside Corners
